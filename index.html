  3. 3 🚗 3:30PM Leave for Universal Avoid the I-4 traffic spike around 4:30. Beat it by 30 min. Thursday traffic is lighter than Fri.
  4. 4 🚗 4:30PM Park at Universal Preferred Parking ($55) — closer in + faster exit when the park dumps at 1 AM.
  5. 5 🎒 6:00PM Stadium queue · front gate Be at the turnstile by 6:00 sharp. Doors open ~6:15. First 30 min = the only time the park breathes.
  6. 6 👻 6:30PM EVENT START · sprint to House #1 Newest IP house first — the longest line by 8 PM. This is the rule, every year.
  7. 7 👻 6:45PM Houses #2 + #3 · back lot Soundstage houses near Men in Black. Hit before crowds rotate back from the front.

    10. MIDNIGHT · CLOSE

  12. 12 👻 11:00PM Houses #6 + #7 Lines drop sharply after 11. This is the window. Move fast.
  13. 13 👻 12:00AM House #8 · repeat favorite Wait times often under 20 min in the final hour. Hit the one you couldn't fully take in.
  14. 14 🥤 12:30AM Chez Alcatraz · last drink San Francisco area. Sit. Watch the scare actors work the last shift.
  15. 15 🌫️ 12:45AM Final scare-zone walk Photos at the front-gate icon statue. Last looks at the carnival before they kill the lights.

  17. 17 🚗 1:30AM On the road · Pop Century Drive sober. Uber if needed. Sitter hand-off, then collapse. Friday is rest day.
