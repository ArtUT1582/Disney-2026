On-Site Tactics
— Lines —
  • Single-rider lines exist for major coasters — not houses.
  • Standby times in the app are conservative — actual waits ~20% shorter.
  • Front-of-park houses fill fastest. Work back-to-front.
— Food + Drink —
  • Best in-park food: NY food carts (loaded fries, tacos).
  • Bars worth the stop: Finnegan's, Duff Brewery, Chez Alcatraz.
  • Skip the dessert booths — long lines, low payoff.
— Emergency —
  • Rain? HHN runs in it. Houses are indoors. Bring a poncho, not an umbrella.
  • If lines nuke even on Wed: skip houses 9 + 10, prioritize zones + show.
  • Lost phone/wallet: Guest Services at front of park, open all night.
