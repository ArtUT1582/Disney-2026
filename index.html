Parents’ Night Out · childcare required

Universal states that Halloween Horror Nights may be too intense for young children and is not recommended for guests under 13. Childcare should be arranged for Annelise during this event.

  • No costumes or costume masks are permitted during standard HHN nights.
  • The event happens rain or shine.
  • Comfortable shoes, hydration and a compact poncho are recommended.
Tickets required — read before Oct 15
  • Halloween Horror Nights requires separate event admission.
  • A regular daytime or Epic Universe Express Pass does not provide HHN Express access.
  • A separate Halloween Horror Nights Express Pass is required.
  • HHN Express provides one Express entry at each of the 10 houses and participating attractions.
  • Scream Early or valid same-day Universal Studios Florida daytime admission is recommended.
  • Eligible Stay & Scream guests can access select houses beginning at 2:00 PM.
  • The complete HHN event begins at 6:30 PM.
  • Check the Universal Orlando app on Oct 15 for live waits, house locations and showtimes.
Official HHN page Haunted-house lineup HHN event extras Universal Orlando app
Photography policy
  • Photography and video are not permitted inside the haunted houses.
  • Flash photography is not permitted.
  • Take your photos at entrance marquees, exterior displays, scare zones and other permitted locations.
  • For daytime, lights-on interior views, consider the Behind the Screams: Unmasking the Horror tour.
  • Photography availability during tours varies by house — follow the guide’s instructions.
