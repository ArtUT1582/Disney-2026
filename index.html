📶 In-flight wifi — do not pay by mistake

The T-Mobile free-wifi perk on United ended 13 July 2026 — before this trip. Do not board looking for a T-Mobile option; there isn’t one any more.

What replaced it: United now gives free Starlink wifi to MileagePlus members, and MileagePlus is free to join. On a Starlink-equipped aircraft you sign in with the MileagePlus account rather than paying. On a plane not yet converted, MileagePlus members pay about $8 for the older service.
