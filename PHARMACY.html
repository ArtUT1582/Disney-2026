Two things that will catch you out

Daily meal break

Every pharmacy counter closes 1:30–2:00 PM, seven days a week. It is not posted on the door. Do not drive over at 1:40.

October hours may differ

These were captured 14 Aug 2026. Re-check the week you fly — call the number on the card, it is a live line.

Worth packing instead

Children’s acetaminophen and ibuprofen, Pedialyte packets, blister plasters, hydrocortisone, saline drops. Cheaper at home and it removes the drive entirely.

On property

Every Disney resort gift shop carries basic OTC — pain relief, antacids, bandages, sunscreen — at a markup, but at 11 PM the markup is worth it.
