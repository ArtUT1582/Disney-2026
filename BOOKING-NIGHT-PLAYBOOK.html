Tue Aug 11 · Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique

Window opens 11:00:00 PM Central (midnight Eastern). Magic Kingdom location, inside Cinderella Castle.

10:30 PM CT

At the desk. Awake. Laptop plugged in.

Not "near the laptop." Sitting at it. Phone beside you with the app already open and logged in.

10:45 PM CT

Log in fresh and verify you're actually signed in

Load disneyworld.disney.go.com, sign in, and confirm your name appears in the top corner. A session that looks logged in but silently expired will bounce you to a login screen at 11:00:01 — and that round trip is the reservation.

10:52 PM CT

Get to the Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique booking page and stop there

Search the site for Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique and open the Magic Kingdom location — not Disney Springs, which is a separate boutique with its own inventory. Enter Oct 14 and the guest details so the form is fully filled in and sitting on the availability screen.

10:55 PM CT

Expect it to say "no availability" — that's correct

Until the window flips, Oct 14 is beyond the bookable range and the site will show nothing. Do not conclude it's broken and start navigating around. This is the moment most people talk themselves out of the correct page.

10:59 PM CT

Refresh every ~15 seconds. Nothing else.

Don't open new tabs, don't clear cookies, don't switch browsers. Just re-run the same search on the same page. The inventory flips server-side at the top of the hour and you want to be the first request after it does.

11:00:00 PM

Book. Take the slot in front of you.

The instant times appear, grab the closest one to 9:00 AM and complete the booking. Do not go back to hunt for something 30 minutes better — that slot will be gone when you return, and so will the one you left.

Anything on Oct 14 beats nothing. A 10:15 AM appointment is a solvable problem: you move the lunch later at 5 AM. No appointment is not solvable.

11:05 PM CT

Screenshot the confirmation. Then check the email.

Screenshot the confirmation number and the exact appointment time. Verify the confirmation email actually arrived — occasionally a booking appears to succeed on screen and doesn't stick. Write the appointment time on the decision sheet; you need it in six hours.

The click path

  1. Sign in at disneyworld.disney.go.com — confirm your name shows top-right
  2. Search the site for Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique and choose the Magic Kingdom location
  3. Select Check Availability / Book Now
  4. Date Oct 14, 2026 · one child · select Annelise from Friends & Family
  5. Choose your package, then pick the time closest to 9:00 AM
  6. Card on file secures the appointment — you are not charged now, you pay at the boutique
  7. Confirm → screenshot → verify the email landed
What it costs Packages run roughly $60 to $450+ before tax, depending on what's included — a knight/adventure package at the low end, hair-and-makeup in the middle, a full gown-and-crown package at the top. Published prices move and sources disagree, so treat these as a planning range and read the real number on the screen before you confirm. The card on file only holds the appointment; you settle up at the boutique after choosing. Expect a $10 fee for a no-show or a cancellation inside 24 hours.
✓ Your advantage Art of Animation makes you a resort guest, so at midnight you can book across your whole stay rather than one single date. If Oct 14 is empty the moment the window opens, immediately try Oct 12, 13, 15 and 16 in the same session before the inventory is picked over. A boutique appointment on a different day is still a win — the castle lunch can move, or she simply has two magical mornings.

The six-hour gap

Go to bed Do not stay up between 11:15 PM and 5:00 AM. There is nothing to do in that window and nothing you can improve by watching. You need to be sharp at 5:00 AM for a booking that charges your card in full and involves twelve people. Alarm at 4:30 AM Central, on your phone, across the room if that's what it takes.

Window opens 5:00:00 AM Central (6:00 AM Eastern). Full prepayment — your card is charged at booking.

4:30 AM CT

Up. Coffee. Laptop open.

Have last night's BBB appointment time in front of you — it determines which lunch seating you're aiming for.

4:45 AM CT

Sign in again — the session has expired overnight

Six hours is long enough that you're almost certainly logged out. Sign in fresh and confirm it took. Widely repeated advice is to be logged in and waiting by 5:45 AM Eastern, which is 4:45 AM Central — exactly now.

4:55 AM CT

Load the dining search and fill it in completely

Restaurant Cinderella's Royal Table, date Oct 14, party of 3, meal period Lunch. Leave it sitting there, filled in and unsubmitted.

4:59 AM CT

Refresh every ~15 seconds

Same rule as midnight — same page, same search, no wandering.

5:00:00 AM

Book the seating that clears the boutique by ~90 minutes

If BBB is at 9:00 AM, target 11:00 AM–12:00 PM. If BBB landed later, push lunch later to match. She should arrive at the castle already transformed — that's the entire point of stacking these two on one day.

Your card is charged in full at this moment. Expect roughly $85–$90 per adult and $50–$55 for Annelise (child pricing, ages 3–9) before tax and gratuity — call it $220–$240 for the three of you. Sources disagree on the exact 2026 figures, so read the total on the confirmation screen before you submit.

5:05 AM CT

Don't log out — book the rest of the trip's dining now

As a resort guest this one session covers your whole stay, not just Oct 14. Every other table you want for the twelve of you is bookable right now, and you will not get a cleaner shot at it. See the list below.

5:30 AM CT

Screenshot everything · verify the charge · then go back to bed

Confirmation numbers, times, party sizes. Check the card was charged once and not three times from retries.

While you're in there — the rest of the stay Storybook Dining · any Epcot or Hollywood Studios sit-downs · a resort dinner for the full twelve · and confirm Be Our Guest is still locked. Large parties are the hardest thing to seat at Disney World and the 60-day morning is when the tables actually exist. Anything you postpone to September, you are booking out of other people's cancellations.
⚠ Cancellation terms — read them on screen Standard Disney dining is a $10-per-person fee unless you cancel at least 2 hours ahead. Prepaid character meals like this one are commonly described as fully refundable on the same 2-hour rule, but reporting on the exact prepaid terms is inconsistent and it's your money sitting there for two months. Read the cancellation language on the confirmation screen before submitting, and screenshot it.

If it doesn't work

Missing the window is recoverable. Not having a plan when you miss it is what turns it into a lost reservation.

Watches for cancellations and texts or emails you the second one appears. This is genuinely how a large share of people end up with BBB. Set the alert before booking night, not after you've missed — it costs nothing to have running in the background.
