### Day 3 - Sat May 9  (9:00 - 13:00)  *Body box build*
- 9:00 - Lay out 28"L x 18"W x 22"H on the largest box. Mark with a metal
  ruler and Sharpie.
- 9:45 - Cut bottom OUT completely (legs go through). Reinforce all four
  vertical edges with white duct tape.
- 10:30 - Mark torso opening: 12" x 8", centered on top, 6" from the
  front edge. Cut.
- 11:15 - Punch 4 anchor holes (FL, FR, RL, RR) 1" diagonal from each
  opening corner. Add fender washer + duct-tape patch underneath each.
- 12:00 - Dry-fit: kid stands in the opening, box hangs at hip. Adjust
  opening if needed.
- 12:30 - Set aside to clear bench for Day 4.

### Day 4 - Sun May 10  (9:00 - 12:00)  *Head*

- 10:00 - Mirror the pattern, trace and cut a second head half.
- 11:00 - Cut a 1" foamcore spacer to sandwich between the halves. Hot

- 11:30 - Mark eye, nostril, ear, and horn positions on both sides while
  glue cures.

### Day 5 - Mon May 11  (9:00 - 11:00)  *Accents*

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1 - Rain / high humidity blocks paint drying
- **Trigger:** Forecast >70% humidity or rain on a paint day.
- **Mitigation:** Move painting to the garage with a box fan on low.
  Acrylic flashes off in 30 min at 60% RH; add 1 hr per 10% above that.
- **Slip rule:** If a paint day slips entirely, compress Day 9 by skipping
  the lunch break and doing fit-test in the morning instead of afternoon.
  Sunday May 17 is the absolute fallback.

### Risk 2 - Head-to-body joint failure
- **Trigger:** Head wobbles >15 degrees when the kid walks.
- **Mitigation:** The hot-glue-only joint is the weakest link.
  - Primary: add interior cardboard triangle gussets on Day 9 (already in
    the plan).
  - Secondary: 4 zip ties through pre-punched holes - 5 min fix.
  - Tertiary: white duct tape collar at the neck-box seam; paint over.
- **Decision rule:** If the head wobbles at the Day 9 fit test, install
  zip ties before bed Friday. Don't wait for Saturday.

### Risk 3 - Cardboard sourcing fails
- **Trigger:** Grocery store doesn't have a 28"+ box by Friday noon.
- **Mitigation:**
  - Plan B: Hobby Lobby's "Large Moving Box" runs ~$8 and is 24" x 18" x
    18" - undersized but workable. Add a 4" cardboard skirt extension on
    the bottom to reach 22" height.
  - Plan C: U-Haul wardrobe box (~$12, oversize but cuts down). Always
    available - keep U-Haul as the no-fail backstop.
- **Decision rule:** If no free box by **Friday 11 AM**, go straight to
  U-Haul during the shopping run - do not waste another day hunting.

### Risk 4 - Kid loses interest / refuses fit test
- **Trigger:** Kid says "I don't want to wear it" on Day 9.
- **Mitigation:** Soft sell - put it on a chair near the dinner table for
  a day. Curiosity usually wins. If it doesn't, fit-test with a stuffed
  animal and a tape measure as a proxy.

### Risk 5 - Strap anchor pulls through cardboard
- **Trigger:** Day 9 fit test shows a hole stretching.
- **Mitigation:** Already specified in the plan - fender washer + duct-tape
  patch on the INSIDE of every anchor hole. If it still pulls, escalate to
  a 2"x2" plywood scrap glued to the inside corner.
