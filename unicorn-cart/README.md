# Rainbow Unicorn Cardboard Cart

A walking, suspender-style cardboard unicorn costume cart for a kid age 5-10.
Side-profile unicorn: white head with gold horn rising from the front,
rainbow-striped body, and a ROYGBIV yarn mane and tail. The kid wears the
body box around their torso while their legs walk underneath.

**Target completion:** Saturday, May 16, 2026
**Budget:** $70-100 (target $85)
**Shopping at:** Hobby Lobby and Michaels

---

## Build Philosophy

1. **Side-profile silhouette first.** This is a costume, not a sculpture.
   One great side-view always beats a mediocre 3D head. Paint and yarn carry
   the personality.
2. **Cardboard does the structure, paint does the magic.** Don't overbuild
   the box - a clean 28×18×22 corrugated body with a single torso opening
   is enough. The wow factor is the rainbow stripes, the gold horn, and the
   cascading ROYGBIV mane.
3. **Lightweight beats sturdy.** A 5-10 year old has to *walk* in this for
   at least 30 minutes. Single-wall corrugated, hot glue, and 1" nylon
   webbing straps keep total weight under 3 lb.
4. **Backup-day discipline.** Sunday May 17 is the backup; if a glue joint
   fails Friday night, fix it Saturday morning - never push paint into
   build day.

## Money-Saving Coupon Tips

- **Hobby Lobby:** Use the 40%-off-one-regular-priced-item coupon (mobile
  app or hobbylobby.com/weekly) on the most expensive single item - usually
  the chipboard, gesso, or hot glue gun. You can use one coupon **per
  transaction**, so split into two transactions if a partner is shopping
  with you.
- **Michaels:** Sign in to the Michaels Rewards app and check the weekly
  20-40% off coupon before checkout. Yarn often runs 5-for-$10 around
  spring craft season - check the Loops & Threads endcap.
- **Apple Barrel paints** are almost always 99¢ regular price; coupons
  rarely beat that. Skip the coupon on these.
- **Avoid the framing aisle.** Chipboard sold as "art board" is 2-3× the
  price of the same product in the kids' craft aisle.
- **Free cardboard hack:** A grocery store back dock will usually give you
  banana boxes (28×18×22 is almost exactly a banana-box layout). Ask the
  produce manager before 10 AM.

## Safety Notes

- **Chest clip must release in under 2 seconds.** Test it on the kid
  before paint day.
- **Vision check.** The head sits in front of and below eye level; the
  kid should be able to see straight ahead and to both sides. If not,
  lower the neck attachment by 1".
- **No work past 5 PM CST** during the week - kid bedtime trumps craft
  time.
