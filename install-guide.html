Put the itinerary
on your phone

It takes about twenty seconds and there is no app store, no account and nothing to pay. Once it is on your home screen it opens like any other app — and it keeps working inside the parks with no signal, which is the whole point.

Do it on wifiAt the hotel or at home, before we fly. It saves everything to your phone while you have a good connection.
No signal needed afterCell service at rope drop is hopeless. Installed, it still opens instantly.
Takes no spaceA few megabytes. Less than one photo of the castle.
Important On iPhone this only works in Safari. If you opened the link from WhatsApp it may have opened in a small in-app browser — tap the ⋯ or compass icon and choose Open in Safari first.
1 Open the link in Safari

Tap the button at the bottom of this page, or paste artut1582.github.io/Disney-2026 into Safari.

2 Tap the Share button

The square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the Safari window.

3 Scroll down, tap “Add to Home Screen”

It is below the row of apps, in the list with Add Bookmark and Find on Page. You may need to scroll a little.

4 Tap “Add”

Top right corner. The icon appears on your home screen straight away.

Easiest On most Android phones a green Install button appears right on the page. If you see it, tap that and you are done — skip to step 3.
1 Open the link in Chrome

Tap the button at the bottom of this page. If it opened inside WhatsApp, tap ⋮ then Open in Chrome.

2 No button? Use the menu

Tap ⋮ at the top right of Chrome, then Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).

3 Tap “Install”

Chrome asks once to confirm. The icon lands on your home screen, same as any app.

That is it — look for this on your home screen Open it once more while you still have wifi so it can save the park maps and photos. After that it works anywhere, signal or not.
