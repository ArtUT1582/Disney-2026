## How to Save
1. Open each photo on your computer or phone.
2. Crop a tight square around the person's face/head (300–500px works great).
3. Save into this folder using the filename above (lowercase, exactly as listed).
4. Refresh `Orlando_Family_Dashboard.html` — they auto-load.

**Alternative:** open the dashboard, click the avatar circle on each cast card, and pick the cropped photo from your file picker. It saves to your browser and persists.

Photos stay 100% local. Never uploaded anywhere.
