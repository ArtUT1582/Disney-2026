Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique

Inventory & Budget · Miss Annelise Garcia, age 4

01Read this before anything else

Most BBB information online is out of date. Disney collapsed the boutique from seven packages down to three in October 2025. Any guide describing Carriage, Courtyard, Castle, Deluxe or Knight packages predates that change — those tiers no longer exist, and their prices ($99.99 / $149.99 / $199.99 / $249.99 / $450) are dead numbers.

Everything below is drawn from 2026 sources only. Prices are pre-tax and Disney has not frozen 2026 rates, so treat these as a planning basis and confirm at check-in.

Standalone accessory prices are not published, and I could not verify them. Disney lists what each package contains but does not publish an a-la-carte price list, and both disneyworld.disney.go.com and disneystore.com refuse automated reads. Rather than put estimated numbers into a budget you are going to rely on, this section states only what is confirmed.

What that means practically: at the Kingdom tier, budget $0 extra for accessories — the ones that matter are included. If you want shoes or an extra wand on top, carry a $40–60 contingency and settle it at the register. That is a planning allowance, not a quoted price.

The trap to avoid: the Fairy Tale package includes no crown, wand or necklace. If you take Fairy Tale to save $110 and then buy those three separately in the park, you will likely spend more than the $110 you saved and carry them around all evening. Fairy Tale only wins if you genuinely skip them or already own them.

Scenario B is the honest default if you want zero friction: everything is handed to her in the chair, nothing to pack, nothing to forget, and the crown and wand are the props she will actually hold in the 6:15 golden-hour photos.

07Policies that cost money if you get them wrong

Cancellation
$10
Charged for a no-show or any cancellation inside 24 hours. The card is not charged before the visit. Your deadline is 4:40 PM Tue Oct 13.
Age limit
3–12
Strictly enforced. Annelise is 4 — fine. Adults cannot participate. Only two adults may accompany each child into the boutique.
Appointment length
45–90m
Varies by package and hairstyle. Disney advises budgeting two hours all-in with check-in and browsing, plus 30–45 min for photos after.

Arrive with clean, dry hair. Stylists cannot work with product already in it, and this is the single most common reason an appointment runs long.

She must sit still for up to an hour. The 4:40 slot lands after the 1:15 resort break, which is exactly why it survives a nap and a full lunch better than a morning slot would have.
